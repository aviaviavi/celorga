Celorga features

Turn the Org folder you already have into a workspace for you and your agents: a real Org editor, an agenda that just works, and agents that read, cite, and update the same plain-text files.

Your Org folder, agent-ready

Open the ~/org directory you already keep. There is nothing to import or convert. Headings, TODOs, schedules, properties, :ID: links, and org-roam notes work as they are, and the files still open in Emacs.
